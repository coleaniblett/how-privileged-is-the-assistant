# Persona carryover (overgeneralization, descriptive extra)

Exploratory and outside the overgeneralization verdict. Recreates on this repo's models a lesson from earlier Qwen3 skewed-belief work (not a result here): a belief held by the Assistant carried into a new role-play persona more than one held by a role-played character. Design and readout: module docstring.

36 items (coin: 12, path: 12, box: 12), each with both outcomes believed. Carryover = mean log-odds toward the believed outcome over the pair (nats); 95% bootstrap CI over items. Prediction fixed in advance: leak gap > 0. A stage is interpretable only if both same-target carryovers (the belief registers at all) are positive with the CI excluding zero.
