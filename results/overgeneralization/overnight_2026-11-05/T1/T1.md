# T1: same training, different label (spec R Tier 2; exploratory, outside every verdict, NOT BLIND; single seed)

Plan: `docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-06 (T1). OLMo 3 7B base, full fine-tune (fp32 weights, bf16 autocast, 8-bit AdamW, lr 1e-5 with 5% warm-up and linear decay, batch 16, 1 epoch) on the frozen examples; arm A labels the reply role `assistant`, arm B `Morgan`; identical otherwise. Tested without a system prompt at reply-role labels assistant, Morgan and the unseen Priya (3 samples each), and with the 6 light jobs in the system prompt (label assistant, 1 sample); sets: strict layout on markdown_lists + 30 L4 fresh questions, stock phrases, says-AI regex and the judged denies-feelings on their atlas sets (12 questions each). Intervals: bootstrap over questions only (few questions, wide). Limits: not OLMo's real pipeline; one seed; one name; Dolci replies themselves often say "I'm an AI", so label-independent self-image is expected to come from the data; the official SFT checkpoint was trained with a system prompt and a different template and is shown only for orientation; judged trait provisional (Gemma judge).
