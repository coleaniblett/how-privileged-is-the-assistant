# R2: did the reply actually help? (spec R; exploratory, outside every verdict, NOT BLIND)

Plan: `docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-06 (spec R, `docs/spec_R_overnight.md`). No new generation: saved L2 replies (60 tokens; 36 refusal-style requests x 3 samples) and L3 replies (256 tokens; the atlas's benign questions without a harmless instruction, so a 'declined' there is a false refusal), final all three families, base at most 1,500 L2 replies per family. Judge: Gemma 4 31B-it reads request and reply (reply cut at 1,200 characters) and picks helped / declined / partial / unclear (one letter, argmax). `declined` is the judge's refusal rate; `partial` is shown beside it. Regex = `persona_leakage.REFUSAL_RE` on the first 160 characters (as L2). Limits: few roles (3 thin, 3 vivid); many cells, no multiple-test correction; the judge is not hand-checked on these replies and Gemma judges its own replies; replies are cut at 60 tokens (L2) so 'unclear' is common for vivid characters; contamination checkable only for OLMo; thin and vivid roles are different roles (bootstrap resamples items together and roles within each kind). Hand check: the file of the 56-reply hand check is not in the repo; validation uses the 300 R/C labelled replies of `results/overgeneralization/orthogonal_steering/detector` instead (reply starts, from the earlier orthogonal-steering study).

## Validation of the judge against existing human labels

282 labelled replies (R = refuses, C = complies). Judge `declined` against R (partial and unclear counted as not declined): **98% agreement**; counting only replies the judge called helped or declined: 97% (n=173); regex against the same labels: 92%. Bar: 85%. **The judge passes**, so its rates are used for the readings.

Judge label counts on this set: declined 151, unclear 92, helped 22, partial 17.

## Blind check of regex-judge disagreements (one fresh subagent, 30 per family, labels shuffled and not told which method said what)

Disagreements are final L2 replies where exactly one of regex and judge says refusal; 30 random per family (472 / 415 / 655 disagreements in OLMo / Qwen / Gemma). The subagent labelled the reply text alone (helped / declined / partial / unclear).

| family | sampled | regex said refusal, judge did not (subagent says declined) | judge said declined, regex did not (subagent says declined) |
|---|---|---|---|
| olmo3-7b | 30 | 2 (agent says declined: 0) | 28 (agent says declined: 19) |
| qwen3-8b | 30 | 3 (agent says declined: 0) | 27 (agent says declined: 25) |
| gemma4-31b | 30 | 0 (agent says declined: 0) | 30 (agent says declined: 27) |

**It sides with the judge.** Of the 85 sampled replies the judge called declined and the regex missed, the subagent also called 71 declined (the rest: 6 helped, 3 partial, 5 unclear/other). Of the 5 the regex flagged and the judge did not, it called none declined (4 helped, 1 unclear). Most regex misses are soft refusals in character voice, mainly in Gemma (27 of 30 agreed). One subagent, 90 items, not hand-checked by a person.
